openplayer plays the music already on your phone. Pick a folder and it becomes a playlist; your files stay exactly where they are.

There are no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no internet access. Nothing you play ever leaves your device.

PLAYBACK
• Gapless playback, or a crossfade of up to 15 seconds between songs
• Even out volume: every song plays at about the same loudness, using ReplayGain tags where a song has them
• Shuffle with a seed you can lock, so a shuffle you like can be played again in the same order
• See and rearrange the next five songs, or swap one out
• Play several playlists at once
• Sleep timer, with the option to finish the current song first
• Skip silence, remember your place in long tracks, and pick up where you left off

YOUR LIBRARY
• Each folder is a playlist, optionally including its subfolders
• Favourites: heart a song from the player or any list, and find them all in one place
• Search every playlist at once, or within one
• Sort by name, artist, length or when the file last changed
• Rename songs in the app without touching the files
• Export your playlists and settings to a file, and import them again
• A small backup is kept in your music folders, so a reinstall can put everything back

SOUND EFFECTS
• Speed, with or without keeping the pitch
• Pitch, in semitones and cents
• Reverb, with presets
• 3D sound: width, distance and position

DESIGNED TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
• A clean, dark interface with only what you need
• Controls on the notification, lock screen and headset buttons
• Optional glass theme, custom background colours and adjustable haptics
• Two-finger brightness and three-finger volume gestures
• Optional fingerprint, face or PIN lock to open the app or delete songs

openplayer is free and open source.
